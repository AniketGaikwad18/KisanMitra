The Fragmented Agricultural Decision Bottleneck

🌿 Crop Disease Silo
Visual leaf symptoms misdiagnosed without agronomist access, leading to ineffective chemical costs.
🧪 Soil Complexity
Lab numbers (pH, NPK) are disconnected from crop-specific split fertilization schedules.
🌦 Raw Weather Numbers
Weather apps show degrees and rain % without farm spray windows or irrigation warnings.
💰 Opaque Mandi Rates
Lack of multi-market wholesale price discovery forces distress selling to middlemen.
🏛️ Dispersed Schemes
Valuable government insurance (PMFBY) and subsidies remain unclaimed across scattered portals.
🌐 Language Barriers
Digital farm tools alienate non-English regional farmers across vernacular states.
⚠️
Core Problem: Fragmented Information ➔ Difficult Decisions ➔ Delayed / Costly Action. “The challenge isn't access to information. It's turning scattered information into one clear farming decision.”
🎙️ Speaker Note: Indian agriculture doesn't suffer from a lack of data—it suffers from disconnected data. When a farmer must make a daily decision, information is scattered across six different places, causing delay, misdiagnosis, and crop loss.
